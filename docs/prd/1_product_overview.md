# Product Overview

### Problem Statement
Most employers include social media screening as part of their pre-employment background checks, but candidates are rarely aware that years of old, often out-of-context content (such as angry tweets posted in frustration, vulgar comments made in casual conversations, or old party photos) can flag them as high-risk hires. This is especially impactful for recent college graduates with 5+ years of social media activity, working professionals switching jobs with 10+ years of digital footprints, and career changers moving to regulated industries where social media screening is standard practice. Currently, there is no accessible, purpose-built tool that lets users audit their own social media presence from an employer’s perspective before applying for roles, leading to unexpected disqualifications and lost job opportunities.

### Solution Summary
ClearRecord AI is a web-based pre-employment privacy tool that empowers job seekers to proactively audit their social media presence for hiring risks. Users upload their official social media data archives from major platforms, and the tool’s AI scans all content against 13 common employer screening risk categories (workplace hostility, aggressive debates, profanity, substance references, etc.). Each flagged item is categorized as High, Medium, or Safe risk, and users can review, delete, or untag risky content directly in the tool. The tool generates a quantifiable Digital Hygiene Score (0-100) and a downloadable PDF report that users can use to demonstrate proactive digital hygiene management to employers.

### Target Audience
1. **Recent College Graduate**
   - Role: Final-year student or 0-2 years into their first full-time role
   - Primary Goals: Clean up immature or unprofessional social media content before applying for jobs
   - Key Needs: Simple, low-effort audit process, clear explanations of what content is flagged and why
   - Pain Points: Unaware of how old teenage/young adult posts will be interpreted by employers, no easy way to review years of scattered social media content
   - Product Support: Guided upload flow, plain-language risk explanations, one-click actions to remove risky content

2. **Working Professional (Job Switcher)**
   - Role: 25-35 year old with 5+ years of professional experience looking to change roles
   - Primary Goals: Identify and remove controversial or unprofessional old content that could disqualify them from new opportunities
   - Key Needs: Support for multiple social platforms, ability to review content in context before deleting, secure handling of personal data
   - Pain Points: Forgot about old controversial posts, worried about past content being taken out of context, no way to see their digital footprint from an employer’s perspective
   - Product Support: Multi-platform archive support, context-aware risk categorization, end-to-end encryption for uploaded data

3. **Career Changer (Regulated Industry)**
   - Role: 30-40 year old moving to a highly regulated industry (finance, healthcare, education, government)
   - Primary Goals: Ensure their entire digital footprint complies with the stricter screening standards of their new industry
   - Key Needs: Granular filtering of risk categories, detailed score breakdowns, employer-facing report options
   - Pain Points: Old content that is harmless in their current industry may be flagged as high-risk in regulated fields, no way to tailor their audit to industry-specific standards
   - Product Support: 13 granular risk categories, category-specific filtering, redacted employer-facing report options

### Core Value Proposition
- Proactively identify hidden hiring risks in your social media history before employers screen you, eliminating unexpected disqualifications
- Get an objective, AI-powered audit categorized by 13 common employer screening risk factors, with clear context for every flagged item
- Take direct action to clean up risky content from within the tool, no need to manually scroll through years of scattered posts across multiple platforms
- Quantify your digital hygiene with a 0-100 score and shareable PDF report to demonstrate proactive privacy management to prospective employers
- Avoid the stress and uncertainty of unknown social media risks by getting full visibility into your digital footprint before applying for roles

### Success Goals
1. 80% of users complete the full audit workflow (upload → scan → review → score generation) within 10 minutes
2. 90% of first-time users identify at least 1 previously unknown risky content item during their initial audit
3. < 2 second average page load time for all core screens on 4G mobile connections
4. 99.5% service availability for core scanning and reporting features
5. 75% of users who complete an audit download the generated PDF report
6. < 1% error rate for the end-to-end social media content scanning workflow
